A Letter From Our Founder

We were asking the wrong question.

Working alongside warehouses and logistics hubs taught me a hard truth: incidents move faster than most systems can respond. Like many, I initially focused on deploying better hardware and higher-resolution cameras.

But after analyzing footage of a real warehouse breach — where a truck forced the perimeter and valuable inventory was gone in minutes — a glaring flaw became impossible to ignore. The cameras worked perfectly. The recording was clear. Yet the loss was total.

Security shouldn't ask, "Did the camera capture it?" It should ask, "How could the environment have responded before the breach escalated?"

That shift in thinking became the foundation of Mellivora. Inspired by the absolute awareness, adaptability, and decisive action of the Honey Badger, our framework treats security not as static hardware on a wall, but as an active, continuously evolving ecosystem.

Our objective is absolute: create more awareness, create more time, and create more options. Time creates the opportunity to intervene, and intervention is what prevents loss.

Let's get to work.

Chris
Founder, Mellivora Systems
